1. Our approach

LunaView respects intellectual-property rights and expects users and partners to do the same. Do not upload, publish, transmit, link to or otherwise make available copyrighted or other protected material without authorization. We may remove or restrict allegedly infringing material after receiving a valid notice, or take other appropriate action.

2. Submit a copyright notice

If you are a rights holder or authorized representative and believe material on the Service infringes your copyright or another right, email bajikorosaki@gmail.com with the subject “LunaView Copyright Notice.” Include: your name and contact information; a clear description of the work or right; the exact location or link to the material; a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized; a statement, where required by law, that the notice is accurate and you are authorized to act; and your physical or electronic signature.

We may forward your notice to the relevant user or third party. False or misleading notices may create legal liability.

3. Counter notice

If your material was removed or restricted and you believe this was in error or you have authorization, send a counter notice to the same address. Identify the material and prior location, provide contact information, state your good-faith belief that removal was mistaken, include any legally required consent to jurisdiction and sign the notice. We will handle counter notices as required by applicable law, but cannot guarantee restoration.

4. Repeat infringement

Where permitted and required by law, we may restrict, suspend or terminate access for repeat infringers or serious violations of this policy.

5. Other content reports

To report fraud, unlawful content, violence, hate, harassment, child-safety risks or other policy concerns, email bajikorosaki@gmail.com with the location, reason and relevant context. For an immediate risk to safety, contact local emergency services.

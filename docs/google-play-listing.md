## Full description

LunaView is a mobile entertainment app for people who enjoy film and video. It focuses on simple discovery, fluid browsing and a comfortable viewing experience.

We believe a great viewing experience should not feel complicated. LunaView uses a clear, intuitive interface to help you browse content, return to where you left off and enjoy a more relaxed rhythm on mobile.

Highlights:

- Clear, intuitive content browsing
- A comfortable mobile-first interface
- Natural management of viewing progress and preferences (where available in the released app)
- Ongoing improvements to reliability and experience

Features, available content and regional availability vary. Refer to the released app and its Google Play listing. By using LunaView, you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## Data safety verification principle

Do not copy this section into Play Console without verification. Complete the form from the final APK/AAB, every SDK (including analytics, crash reporting, advertising, sign-in and payments) and server logs.

| If actually collected | Common Play data category | Common purpose, only if true |
|---|---|---|
| Email address or display name | Personal info | App functionality, Account management |
| Viewing progress, favorites or searches | App activity | App functionality, Analytics, Personalization |
| IP, device model, app version or crash reports | App info and performance / Device or other IDs | Analytics, Fraud prevention / Security, App functionality |
| Advertising ID or ad interactions | Device or other IDs / App activity | Advertising or marketing, Analytics |
| Order or subscription status | Financial info, depending on actual data | App functionality, Account management |

For every applicable data type, confirm whether it is collected, shared, optional, encrypted in transit and deletable on request. Resolve any conflict between the Play declaration and the privacy policy before submission.

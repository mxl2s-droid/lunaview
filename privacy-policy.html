3. How we use information

We may use information to operate and personalize the Service; preserve settings and viewing progress; authenticate accounts; protect users and prevent fraud or misuse; diagnose crashes and improve performance; respond to support requests; comply with legal obligations; and, where applicable and permitted by law, deliver or measure advertising.

4. Sharing, advertising and third parties

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that host, analyze, secure, support or deliver the Service, and they may process it only under appropriate instructions. If the released app displays ads, advertising partners may process identifiers, IP address and device information for delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement under their own policies. Google Play processes purchases under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition or sale of assets. Any advertising seller must be reflected in our app-ads.txt once a real publisher ID is configured.

5. Retention

We keep information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described here, to resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data category and purpose. We then delete, anonymize or securely isolate information as appropriate.

6. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction, deletion or export of your personal information; withdraw consent; object to or restrict certain processing; and make choices about direct marketing or personalized advertising. You can reset or limit advertising personalization through your device settings. This does not stop ads entirely, but may affect their relevance.

Email bajikorosaki@gmail.com with the subject “LunaView Privacy Request,” your request and the account email used (if any). We may verify your identity before acting. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, such as access controls, encryption in transit where supported and least-privilege practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please protect your device and account credentials.

8. Children

LunaView is not directed to children below the minimum digital-consent age in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information without appropriate consent. If you believe a child provided us information improperly, contact us so we can review and act as required by law. Parents and guardians should consider content ratings before permitting use.

9. International processing

Information may be processed outside your country by us or our providers. Data-protection laws may differ. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as contractual protections or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Service, technology or legal requirements change. For material changes, we will provide notice through the app, website or another reasonable method and update the date above.
